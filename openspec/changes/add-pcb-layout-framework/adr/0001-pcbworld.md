# ADR 0001: PCBWorld

**Status:** Accepted (2026-09-06)
**RFC:** 11 §4.2 (routing corpus and metrics), §13.1 track B, Appendix C.2 item 7

## Context

RFC 11 §4.2 says "adopt PCBWorld corpus, splits, and its eight metrics verbatim" for external comparability. The teardown of `LGAI-Research/PCBWorld` at `909884e` (5 September 2026) found that the project is not what that sentence assumes.

## Evidence

- **Two programs, two licenses.** The environment, agents, and evaluation code are under the *PCBWorld License 1.0-NC*. Its §3.1 prohibits using "the Software, Derivatives, or Output for any commercial purposes, including but not limited to, developing or deploying products, services, or applications". §1.3 defines Output as "any data, results, PCB layouts, routed designs, logs, evaluation metrics, or other materials generated by the Software". The engine (`PCBWorld-Engine`, KiCad 9.0.8's push-and-shove router behind pybind11, served over a unix socket) is GPL-3.0 in a separate repository, and the README forbids building any combined artifact. The authors state a permissive relicense is "under consideration".
- **No corpus is distributed.** Synthetic boards are generated locally by the environment (`synth_2L_v2`, 10 000 boards in the paper); the real-board set "D3" is 679 boards derived from `PCBench/PCBench` by a KiCad 5 to 9 conversion and a DRC repair/filter pipeline whose scripts live in the NC-licensed repository. The split files (`configs/datasets/d3.json`) list PCBench board ids under a `NNNN_` prefix.
- **The eight metrics** (paper §4.3, `eval/README.md`): Clean Pass (CP, primary: full connectivity and zero DRVs on the selected rollout), Potential Gain (Pot., change in the reward potential from bare board to routed), Routability (Rout., `Σ(Gᵢ(0) − Gᵢ(t)) / Σ(Gᵢ(0) − 1)` over pad connectivity groups per net), DRV count, total wirelength (WL), via count (Via), wall-clock time, and Parse-fail (share of outputs that do not load as `.kicad_pcb`). The protocol is best-of-five rollouts selected by potential gain ("@5"), all metrics on the selected rollout except Time (mean of five) and Parse-fail (all five); deterministic methods run once. DRV counting has two modes: errors only (canonical) and errors plus three promoted warnings (dangling via, dangling track, net conflict). Potential Gain depends on their reward configuration (`drc_dense_errors_only_eval`), which is code, not a definition.
- The evaluation pipeline is three stages: rollout, post-hoc DRC through their engine, aggregation to `per_boards_{ckpts,overall,summary}.csv`.

## Decision

1. **Adopt the metric definitions and the protocol as facts; import no code.** copperhead computes CP, Rout., DRV (both counting modes), WL, Via, Time, and Parse-fail from their published definitions, on KiCad 10's own DRC through `kicad-cli`. Potential Gain is *not* adopted: it is a function of PCBWorld's reward code. Track B reports the seven definable metrics and states that Pot. is omitted and why.
2. **Selection.** The bench's default selection is post-hoc DRC-aware (fewest DRVs, then completion, then wirelength), and the @5 protocol is reproduced with five seeds for seeded engines and one run for deterministic ones. A report labels which selection it used; numbers are comparable to the paper's only when the selection and the DRC engine match, which they do not exactly (KiCad 10 versus 9.0.8), and the report says so.
3. **No PCBWorld software runs in or beside copperhead**, no synthetic boards are generated with it, and no D3 processed boards are used, because all three are Output or Derivatives under the NC license and copperhead is an Apache-2.0 product. The real-board corpus is PCBench directly (ADR 0003), converted with `kicad-cli pcb upgrade`, which is what our own tooling does anyway.
4. **`router-pcbworld-pns` stays a candidate**, not part of v1: the engine is GPL-3.0 and could run out of process like FreeRouting, but its socket protocol is defined by the NC-licensed environment, so a wrapper would have to be written from the engine's own sources alone. Revisit if the relicense lands.

## Consequences

- RFC 11 §4.2's "verbatim" becomes "definitions verbatim, code none"; the RFC's wording is corrected at its next revision.
- Track B comparability with the paper is approximate and labelled; exact parity would need the paper's engine and reward code, which the license forbids in this product.
- The 32 small DRC-clean PCBench boards (ADR 0003) are a smaller qualification set than PCBWorld's 679, and the difference is stated in every report.
