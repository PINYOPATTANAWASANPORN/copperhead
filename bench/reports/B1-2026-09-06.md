# B1 — routing portfolio baseline

Milestone B1 of RFC 11 §13.5 (`openspec/changes/add-pcb-layout-framework`, task 3.8). Two independent routing engines behind the one contract, both suites runnable, identical verification for every candidate, and the three B1 numbers: selection regret, invalid-over-valid rate, orchestration overhead. Clean-pass and quality uplift are reported, not claimed.

- Date: 2026-09-06
- Harness: copperhead 0.10.0 at `0f4fd25`, kicad-cli 10.0.4, node v24.18.0, linux x64
- Engines (both out of process, both re-verified by the harness):
  - `router-freerouting` 2.4.1 — GPL-3.0-only, JRE 25, nondeterministic (no seed control in the CLI)
  - `router-kicad-tools` 0.20.0 (`kct route`) — MIT, seeded
- Mode: `ensemble` (every eligible engine runs; the harness ranks); scoring profile `default-low-speed-2-layer`
- Suites: `bench/suites/microboards.json` (10 golden microboards; the RFC target of 40 is not met yet, see "Gaps") and `bench/suites/pcbench-qual.json` (20 PCBench boards, MIT corpus at `dec3be7`, recorded-permissive, two-layer, DRC-clean, under 50 footprints; ADR 0003)

Regret and invalid-over-valid are zero by construction of the ranking (`src/pcb/verify/scoring.ts`: hard gates, then lexicographic completion, then the weighted score, with the Pareto frontier kept) and are measured here rather than assumed: the bench recomputes them from the recorded candidates.

What the table shows:

- **Identical verification for every engine.** kicad-tools' `clearance` and `fixed-connector` candidates are 100 % routed by the engine's own account and fail the harness gate on KiCad clearance errors; Freerouting's `keepout` candidate is complete and fails on `items_not_allowed`. Neither engine's self-report is trusted.
- **Selection is not "the strongest engine".** kicad-tools wins `decoupling-far` (0 vias against Freerouting's 2, wirelength second under the return-path-first profile); Freerouting wins the rest it completes. This is the portfolio effect the RFC asks for; it is one board out of ten, so no uplift claim is made.
- **Freerouting's neck-down** below the class width at small pads is widened to the class width before verification and recorded as `quality.engine_neckdown_widened`; without it every Freerouting candidate on the SOIC boards failed `track_width`.

What the corpus run shows:

- **Zero refusals after the pre-flight tolerances landed.** The first pass refused five of these boards on a 0.4 µm imperial annular shortfall, a 2.5 µm outline gap, and a slotted barrel-jack hole measured on the wrong axis. All three were harness bugs, found by real boards and fixed with tests; every board now reaches an engine.
- **Freerouting completes 12 of 20 author-placed boards DRC-clean with no human help.** Its remaining candidates are gated out on real defects (clearance and shorts on the dense `rufs_smart_psu`, `hp_led_switch`, `piezo_amplifier` boards) or left connections owed. `AnalogThermometer` is complete and fails on one clearance error: the gate holds even when the engine is one violation from clean.
- **kicad-tools rarely competes on real boards.** Its A* runs to the 300 s budget on the eight larger boards and, where it finishes, its candidates carry clearance errors or low completion. It wins one board (`crossover`, both engines at 67 %, fewer vias and less wire). The dialect fix made it run at all: on the first pass it saw zero nets on every KiCad 10 file and reported nothing.
- **The back-side fix is visible.** `spisolator` (a SOIC on the back) went from five shorts and thirteen critical DRC errors to a clean PASS once images were presented from the top view as KiCad's own exporter does.
- Engine seconds dominate: the harness adds under 4 s per board (import, snapshot, two zone refills, two KiCad DRC runs, ranking) against a mean of 40 engine seconds.

## Gaps against the milestone text

- The microboard suite has 10 boards, not 40. The remaining 30 (RFC §13.3 categories) are queued; the runner, reports, and the suite format do not change when they land.
- `kicad-tools` has no `--seed` in `kct route`; its "seeded" determinism is by construction of the algorithm, not by an argument. Freerouting is nondeterministic; seed variance on it is reported by `copperbench run --seeds 0,1,2` and is not part of this report.
- Overhead is measured on a shared machine while other engines ran; it is an upper bound.

## Reproduce

```bash
bench/corpora/tools.sh          # JRE 25, freerouting-2.4.1.jar, kicad-tools venv under bench/var/tools
bench/corpora/pcbench.sh        # PCBench at dec3be7, qualification boards upgraded under bench/var/corpora
copperbench run bench/suites/microboards.json --track e --out bench/var/runs/B1-microboards
copperbench run bench/suites/pcbench-qual.json --routers router-freerouting,router-kicad-tools --mode ensemble --track b --budget-seconds 300 --out bench/var/runs/B1-pcbench-qual
```

The HTML reports (`report.html` in each run directory) carry the full RFC 11 §13.4 record: versions, licenses, adopted-versus-built layers, per-board raw metrics, failure counts, and the reproduction command.
